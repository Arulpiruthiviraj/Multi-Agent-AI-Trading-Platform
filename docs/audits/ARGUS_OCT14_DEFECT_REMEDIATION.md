## Not fixed (and why)
* Calibration freshness / grader starvation, clustered-count calibration, STRONG-vs-MODERATE trust asymmetry, lineage fate for absorbed ideas: documented in `ARGUS_DISCOVERY_AND_CONSENSUS_FORENSICS.md`. They touch calibration data flow or the protected `ChiefTraderAgent`; no proven defect justifies a same-night change that would alter approvals.
* Swap pacing: production hard-caps swaps at one per cycle at full capacity (`Math.min(momentumHotSwapSlotsPerCycle, 1)`), so the proposed counterfactual knob (2/3) has no effect; the experiment was invalid, not evidence about pacing. Reported `SWAP_CAP_REACHED` ~4.2k per 390-minute session.
