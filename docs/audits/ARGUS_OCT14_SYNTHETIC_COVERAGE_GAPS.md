## 3. Why each earlier layer of certification "worked" and still missed this

* The safety oracles (accounting, risk, lineage, restart 14/14, soak 77/0) are real and independent — and they answer a different question (does it stay safe when it trades). They contain **no assertion that a legitimate opportunity becomes a trade**, and the sessions that trade use a confidence distribution (uncalibrated) that production does not produce.
* The discovery work (cert/universal-discovery) is real, but isolated from the sessions (mocked provider, 12 slots by default) and never asserted anything.
* The release gate treated `STRATEGY_AUTHORIZATION` as informational. A gate that reports "no strategy can emit" and still returns PASS cannot detect a Quant outage.
